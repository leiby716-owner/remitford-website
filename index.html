GOOD TO KNOW

You can always claim directly.

State unclaimed property programs allow owners to search for and claim their property directly, generally without paying a finder. Remitford is a private business, not a government office. Service availability and any paid assistance are subject to applicable registration, licensing and legal requirements and a separate written agreement.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Not sure where to begin? We're here to explain the process.

What is unclaimed property?+

It may include uncashed checks, dormant accounts, or other assets that have been turned over to a state unclaimed property program.

Is Remitford part of the government?+

No. Remitford is a private business. You can search state databases and file eligible claims directly through government websites.

Is recovery guaranteed?+

No. The state determines whether a property belongs to a claimant and whether documentation is sufficient. No recovery or timeframe is guaranteed.

How much does assistance cost?+

Any fee must be disclosed in a separate written agreement and comply with applicable law. Contact us to discuss your circumstances; no fee is charged merely for sending an inquiry.

Should I send my ID or Social Security number here?+

No. Please do not send sensitive identity documents, bank information, Social Security numbers, or account numbers through this website or ordinary email.
