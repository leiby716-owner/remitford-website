REMITFORD PROPERTY RECOVERY

A clear path, step by step.

The state decides whether a claim is payable. We help explain the preparation and review process.

01

Identify a possible match

Start with a potential unclaimed property record. Verify the property ID and basic details using the state's official website. A similar name alone does not prove ownership.

02

Confirm eligibility

Understand the state's rules and who may submit the claim. Claims for estates, trusts, and businesses can require additional paperwork.

03

Gather supporting documents

Collect identification and records requested by the state, such as address history or evidence linking you to the property. Share sensitive documents only through an appropriate secure process.

04

Submit to the state

Follow the state's own submission instructions. For legally authorized investigator-assisted claims, the required written agreement and claim forms may apply.

05

Wait for the state's decision

Review times vary. Only the state determines ownership, requests additional evidence, and approves or denies payments.

Prefer to do it yourself?

You can search and file directly with official state programs without hiring Remitford. A good starting point is USA.gov's unclaimed money guide ↗.
