Remitford is a private unclaimed property assistance initiative focused on making complicated administrative processes easier to understand.

WHO WE ARE

A thoughtful approach to property recovery.

Old bank accounts, outstanding checks and other unclaimed assets can be difficult to identify and claim. Each state has its own rules, and requests for documentation can be confusing.

Remitford's aim is to help people approach that process with organized information, clear communication and attention to detail. We believe a property owner should understand the official process and their option to file a claim directly, without paying a private finder.

Clarity first

Plain-language explanations without pressure.

Respect for privacy

No requests for SSNs or sensitive documents through the public contact form.

No false promises

No guarantee of recovery, eligibility or processing time.

Independence and compliance

Remitford is not a government agency, and it is not affiliated with a state controller or treasury office. Services and representation are contingent on meeting applicable regulatory requirements. This website does not represent that a registration or license has already been granted.
