Learn the basics about unclaimed property, fees, privacy, and government claim decisions.

What counts as unclaimed property?+

Depending on the state, it can include uncashed checks, dormant accounts, security-related assets and other funds turned over to a government unclaimed property program.

Can I file a claim myself for free?+

Yes. Official state programs generally let owners search and submit claims directly without hiring a finder. Start with your state's official unclaimed property website.

Does finding my name guarantee the money is mine?+

No. Name matches may be coincidental, records may be old, and the state will require evidence establishing rightful ownership.

Is Remitford a government office?+

No. Remitford is a private business and is not affiliated with any government agency.

How much will it cost?+

Submitting an inquiry is free. Any available paid assistance requires a separate written agreement stating the fee and is subject to the laws of the relevant state. No fee or service is promised through this website.

How long does a claim take?+

It varies by state, property type and the completeness of supporting information. Only the government program can provide claim-specific status or decisions.

Can I upload an ID or financial documents here?+

No. Please don't send SSNs, bank account numbers, identity documents, or other sensitive data through the form or ordinary email.

Can Remitford file on my behalf now?+

Representation is available only after all applicable investigator, registration, licensing and other requirements have been satisfied. Contact us about the current availability of services.
