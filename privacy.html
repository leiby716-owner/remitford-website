Your privacy matters.

A plain-language explanation of the information this website handles.

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Information you choose to provide

This website is a static site. The contact form uses an email link to open your email program with your message prepared. The website itself does not save your form information to a database or send it directly to our server. If you click Send in your own email app, your email provider and ours handle that message under their respective terms and security practices.

What we ask for

The inquiry form asks for your name, email, optional phone number and a short general message. Please do not include Social Security numbers, account numbers, identity documents, dates of birth or sensitive financial records.

How information may be used

If you email us, we may use your contact information to respond to your inquiry, discuss a potential service, and retain related correspondence as reasonably necessary for operations, legal compliance and records.

Cookies and analytics

This static package does not include analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or site cookies. A hosting service, if one is added later, may collect ordinary server logs. This policy should be reviewed before any new services are enabled.

External links

Links to government websites and email applications are outside our control. Their privacy practices may differ.

Questions and contact

Email leo@remitford.com or call (929) 627-3221 about privacy inquiries.

Before publication: This is a starter privacy policy based on the current static functionality. Review it with qualified counsel and update it if you add hosting integrations, online submissions, analytics, payments or customer portals.
